# Yennefer & Triss

Playable import of [the UmDb Realm’s Fall deck](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/yennefer-triss), retrieved 2026-10-28. The unmodified API payload, retrieval timestamp, notes and complete printed inventory are in [inventory.json](yennefer-triss/inventory.json). The [publisher’s set rules](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/UM-W-RF-Set-Rules.pdf) clarify hero selection, health, immediate ordering, Triss’s mandatory friendly targeting and ongoing schemes.

The deck preserves **30 cards across eleven designs**. Both fighters are ranged with movement 2. Before opening hands, each owner chooses Yennefer or Triss as the 14-HP hero; the other becomes a 6-HP sidekick. Defeating the chosen hero eliminates the owner. Named card eligibility follows the fighter’s identity, independent of role. Only the chosen hero has an active special.

## Clarifications and shared conventions

No deck quantities, printed combat/BOOST values, fighter restrictions or source effects are omitted. The source note that only the chosen hero’s special is active is enforced for both roles. Mandatory adjacent damage can hit the friendly sidekick when it is the only adjacent fighter.

“Simultaneously” uses sealed commitments requested in seat order: later owners cannot see earlier selections, and no draw or reaction starts until all choices finish. Exact commitments, receipts and nested continuations survive JSON recovery. Spectators and other owners receive no private options. Cards stay in their owners’ hands throughout this reveal.

Effective BOOST belongs to the card owner. Advisor applies to Maneuver, attack/defense BOOSTs and effects reading that owner’s BOOST values. Combat BOOST amounts are captured when paid, so cancellation, replacement and public receipts retain the actual amount. The printed card display remains unchanged.

The project’s shared rules remain in force: five opening cards, two actions, hand limit seven, two exhaustion damage per missing draw to each living owned fighter, no automatic reshuffle, free-for-all four-seat matches and existing combat-defeat resolution. Hero selection happens after starting spaces are assigned but before any opening cards, loadout decisions or ally placement; this preserves the information boundary of choosing at game start.

## Original artwork

Fifteen original assets were made with integrated Codex Imagegen on 2026-10-28: two identity portraits, two genuinely transparent full-body figures, and eleven distinct cinematic card scenes. Published deck artwork was not redistributed. Both portraits serve as identity references. Selected originals, exact prompts, generator paths, SHA-256 hashes and reference copies are in `artwork/characters/yennefer-triss/`.

`pnpm artwork:prepare yennefer-triss` generates local hashed WebP derivatives and canonical card URLs. The source manifest controls responsive crops and figure anchors. Hero/sidekick portraits follow actual selected identities; owner colors, numbered sidekick labels and board effects remain separate from the raster artwork.
