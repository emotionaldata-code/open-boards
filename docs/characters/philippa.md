# Philippa

Imported from [UmDb’s Philippa deck](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/philippa), retrieved **2026-10-28** from its public JSON endpoint. The source identifies the set as “Realm’s Fall”; the publisher calls it **The Witcher: Realms Fall**. The [unmodified source inventory](philippa/inventory.json) retains all source text, notes, image reference URLs and retrieval provenance. No card/deck notes were supplied.

Philippa has **12 HP**, **movement 2**, ranged attacks and one **6-HP melee Dijkstra**. The deck preserves **30 copies across 13 designs**, every printed value, BOOST, eligibility restriction and timing. No rules have been omitted or intentionally adapted.

Two Steps Ahead offers one optional end-step draw to four only when the hand is smaller than four. Accepting computes one draw of the shortfall, retaining normal draw reactions and exhaustion. It cannot be used at four or more cards. This follows the [publisher’s set rules](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/UM-W-RF-Set-Rules.pdf), which also confirm one ongoing scheme per owner and end-of-owner-turn upkeep checks. Polymorphy’s printed movement change applies while active; the rulebook’s example describes it at the start of subsequent turns without limiting the ongoing effect to those turns.

## Shared conventions and implementation

The engine retains its five-card opening hand, two-action turns, seven-card hand limit, two exhaustion damage per missing draw to each living owned fighter, free defense and current elimination conventions. This is a faithful deck import into those shared game rules, not a claim that all physical-game edge cases have changed. Free-for-all “opponent” choices can target any living opposing player; combat clauses bind the actual combat opponent.

New reusable vocabulary is documented in [the engine guide](../engine.md): `draw-to`, `maneuvered`, selected own-zone area damage, opponent-chosen partial hand reveal/discard, ongoing movement replacement/zone upkeep, and a typed printed-value expression for the existing card-value lock. Every continuation is serializable and server-authoritative. Reveal selection is presented as four distinct private selections before revealing the completed subset; its order has no rules effect. Private choices expose neither the unselected hand nor server continuations to other viewers. Ordinary ongoing discard protection may waive the discard after the required reveal; the later draw still occurs.

## Artwork provenance

Original assets use integrated Codex Imagegen and the repository’s cinematic illustrated-realism direction. Philippa retains her black braided hair, burgundy blindfold, plum gown, feathered mantle and owl motif; Dijkstra is the heavy-set bald Redanian spymaster in green brocade. No source card art is served or redistributed. Source URLs are retained for audit only.

Selected originals, exact prompts, identity references and tool/date provenance live in [`artwork/characters/philippa/`](../../artwork/characters/philippa/). There are two portraits, two transparent figures and thirteen distinct card scenes. `pnpm artwork:prepare philippa` creates local hashed derivatives and registers canonical card images. Owner colors, ally numbering and board pedestals remain separate from the generated figures.
