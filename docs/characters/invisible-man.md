# Invisible Man

Imported from [UmDb: Invisible Man](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/invisible-man), Cobble & Fog, retrieved **2026-10-26** through its public JSON endpoint. The [source inventory](invisible-man/inventory.json) retains the retrieval timestamp, source URL, unmodified deck payload, every printed timing and BOOST, source image references and setup clarification.

Invisible Man has **15 HP, movement 2, melee attacks, no sidekicks and three fog tokens**. His deck has **30 copies across 14 designs**. Every card is restricted to Invisible Man (`MAIN`). Tokens are public board objects with stable owner and number; they are not fighters, cannot attack or be attacked, do not block fighters, and never share a space with another fog token. Setup places them in distinct empty spaces in the hero's starting zone, after fighter deployment and before first-turn abilities. Deployment reserves sufficient empty spaces for each owner's tokens without reducing anyone's sidekick count.

The passive adds one only when defending with an actual defense/versatile card on owned fog; it survives cancellation of card effects. Owned fog spaces are adjacent **for ordinary movement only**, including effect-granted movement. They do not grant attack reach, shared zones, push/pull shortcuts or access to another Invisible Man's network. Fog moves through fighters and tokens using normal movement connections, including passages; it cannot use the hero-only fog links or stop on another fog.

## Shared conventions and clarifications

No printed rule is omitted and no deck adaptation was requested. The [publisher’s Cobble & Fog rulebook](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/UM-Cobble_and_Fog-rules-two-page.pdf) clarifies token exclusivity and normal movement connections. The [officially endorsed rulings archive](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13b-FbPq_vuqcc3IokeHvQ2ctJaDNZZuUaZmt4uft5h0/edit) clarifies dormant players, Vanish/Emerge and Arthur’s BOOST. These were checked on 2026-10-26. The raw UmDb note’s “other tokens” means other token types; the printed rule explicitly forbids a second fog token on one space. Open Boards retains its shared five-card opening hand, two actions, seven-card hand limit, exhaustion and free-for-all conventions. Source schemes print value one; their unused action combat value normalizes to zero. Printed BOOST values are preserved independently.

In combat, “your opponent” is the exact other participant's owner. Step Lightly is a scheme: the owner chooses an opposing player for its separate fog-movement clause in a four-seat free-for-all. The source solo hero has no friendly adjacent pieces to damage. “Move to a space with a fog token” uses placement without a path or distance, while ordinary “up to” movement retains Stay. Fog may share spaces with fighters or other token types, but never another fog token, including fog owned by another player. Initial deployment disallows fighters and other owned tokens; fog remains nonblocking thereafter.

Temporary removal preserves health, ownership and living status. At the end of the action, a player without any fighters on the board becomes dormant: that player cannot act, draw, discard, be chosen as an opponent or take off-board fighter damage. Vanish therefore also skips the dormant player's hand-limit discard. Return placement happens before other start hooks, but the new turn's position/token snapshots retain the initial off-board position, so returning onto fog does not enable Emerge From Mist. Cancellation preserves effects already resolved and prevents pending effects/continuations belonging to the suppressed card.
