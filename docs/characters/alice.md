# Alice

Source: [Alice on UmDb](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/alice), public JSON retrieved **2026-10-27** from `https://unmatched.cards/api/db/decks/alice`. Edition: **Battle of Legends, Volume One**. The [unmodified source snapshot and complete inventory](alice/inventory.json) retain every source clause, note, artwork reference and retrieval timestamp. No source notes were supplied.

Alice has **13 HP**, movement **2**, melee attacks and one **8-HP melee Jabberwock**. The deck preserves **30 copies across 15 designs**, each printed BOOST and MAIN/ALLY/BOTH restriction. Source scheme `value: 2` is a database placeholder, normalized to engine action value 0; BOOST remains separate and unchanged.

Curiouser and Curiouser uses the reusable `game-start` hook after main fighters occupy their seat starts, **before opening hands, loadout choices, ally placement or turn-start effects**. Each Alice chooses BIG or SMALL independently in seat order; no opening cards are exposed before the decision. The existing private choice protocol supports JSON recovery, stale/forged rejection and spectator isolation.

Persistent reusable `big` and `small` statuses represent the public size marker. BIG adds 2 in the attacker role; SMALL adds 1 in the defender role only when a defense/versatile card is committed. ANY and versatile cards qualify according to their current role. Jabberwock receives no bonus. Status abilities preserve character bonuses through card cancellation and card-only value locks; final value ignoring/locks retain their ordinary precedence. Changing size removes the old status before applying its opposite; movement and drawing finish before the flip. Size does not change occupied spaces, movement, targeting, health or fighter role.

The shared `choose-option.count` field implements Looking Glass without duplicated option trees. Invalid counts are rejected during definition validation; duplicate/unknown choices, too few/many selections, wrong owners and expired prompts reject atomically. Selection order is preserved across nested continuations and reconnects. A full-health heal remains a legal, distinct choice.

## Conventions and artwork

No deck adaptation or omitted rule is intended. Ordinary Open Boards free-for-all elimination, owner-based hand/deck, matching-based ally deployment, hand limit seven, two-action turns and no automatic reshuffling still apply. Mandatory effects continue through their serialized queue under the engine’s existing owner-elimination rules. Other-space placement uses empty endpoints and excludes the current space.

Original artwork is generated with integrated Codex Imagegen using the repository’s cinematic illustrated realism direction. Dedicated portraits establish Alice’s blond hair, black ribbon, blue Victorian dress, ivory apron and vorpal sword, and the Jabberwock’s teal scales, copper throat, whiskers and hooked claws. Identity references are reused for both transparent figures and all fifteen distinct card scenes. Source illustrations are retained as provenance links only and are not redistributed.

Masters, exact prompts, identity references, provenance and preparation inputs live in [`artwork/characters/alice/`](../../artwork/characters/alice/). `pnpm artwork:prepare alice` produces local hashed WebP derivatives and canonical card references. Pedestals, owner colors, numbered ally labels and animation remain shared board presentation.

Desktop player badges give ally details the full second row so the Jabberwock’s name and health do not overlap movement, cards or hero health in a four-player match. Keyboard/touch choices, numbered Jabberwock targets, all four owner colors, card inspectors and reduced/full motion are covered by the Alice browser scenarios.
