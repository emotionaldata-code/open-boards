# Sinbad

Imported from [Unmatched Database: Sinbad](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/sinbad), Battle of Legends, Volume One, retrieved **2026-10-29**. The [unmodified API snapshot](sinbad/source.json) records the retrieval timestamp and public endpoint. The [complete inventory](sinbad/inventory.json) preserves sixteen designs, thirty copies, printed values, explicit BOOSTs, restrictions, every timed clause, source image URLs and source notes (none supplied).

Sinbad has **15 HP**, melee attacks and base movement **2**, with one **6-HP melee Porter**. Seven distinct VOYAGE cards retain their tag. No printed rule is omitted or intentionally adapted. Riches Beyond Compare retains the source’s scheme value 2, although schemes do not resolve combat values.

`maneuverBonus.perDiscardTag` reads the owner’s live public discard and increases the common Maneuver allowance for Sinbad and the Porter. “May move +1” increases their maximum legal distance; moving fewer spaces or staying remains legal. It never increases a distance printed on Toil and Danger, the Cannibals Voyage or Leap Away. A Voyage paid as a Maneuver BOOST enters discard before either fighter moves: even a printed zero BOOST adds one space through 7 Voyages. Voyage Home itself has BOOST one.

`{ stat: 'discard', tag: 'voyage' }` excludes unfinished combat commitments, so a Voyage never counts itself. `recover-card` with `tag` and `all` transfers every matching earlier instance at once without a selection prompt. It excludes the resolving Voyage Home, performs no deck draw or exhaustion, and drops movement immediately. Home settles into discard at combat completion, leaving one Voyage there. Cancellation removes both the Voyage attack bonus and its after-combat clause, preserving printed value and 7 Voyages.

Hand inspection and movement use ordinary private serializable continuations. The combat opponent reference resolves the actual participant’s owner even when that participant is an ally. The Porter shares his owner’s deck, hand and action budget. Committed owner-level draws survive his defeat; defeated fighters cannot move or heal. Existing conventions apply: free defense, defense wins ties, shared hand limit, turn-start position tracking, exhaustion and elimination.
