## Continuous state and shared conventions

`allies-defeated` requires at least one owned ally and every owned ally to be defeated. Off-board living allies are not defeated. Conditional character modifiers supply +1 attack/defense and +1 base movement. Their combat bonuses read live state after during-combat effects, so sacrificing or reviving the final Rider changes the current combat. No persistent ENRAGED counter can become stale. Combat bonuses require an actual combat card and respect ignored values and total-value locks; a card-only value lock preserves the character ability. Movement is shared with the public HUD and authoritative Maneuver range.

Defeat is not damage: sacrifice bypasses prevention and resolves ordinary elimination reactions before its exact continuation. Friendly Rider choices cannot select an enemy, the hero, or a defeated Rider. Revival restores the same numbered piece with printed HP, fresh statuses/counters and its existing usage history. Normal empty-space and movement-lock conventions still apply. Portal Defense’s original card restriction is checked when committed, then its explicit replacement transfers that card to the Rider. A failed exchange does not transfer defense.

All optional and nested choices use existing private, serializable continuations. Face-up attack is an explicit validated command flag, copied into the combat frame and projected publicly before defense. Choosing face-down preserves normal secrecy and range. Cancellation cannot undo completed targeting permission. Damage to several fighters snapshots the union of affected spaces; multiple adjacent friendlies do not multiply damage. Multiplayer uses existing enemy-owner, defender-first timing and per-seat-turn conventions. The map remains King Solomon’s Mine; no Realm’s Fall battlefield was imported.

## Artwork

All 15 assets are original images generated with integrated Codex Imagegen on 2026-10-28: two identity portraits, two genuine transparent full-body figures and eleven individual card illustrations. Public source card images were retained only as provenance URLs and were not redistributed. The masters, portrait identity references, exact prompts and provenance are under `artwork/characters/eredin/`. [Prompts](../../artwork/characters/eredin/prompts.json) and [provenance](../../artwork/characters/eredin/provenance.json) record the production method. Each scene follows the repository’s cinematic illustrated realism direction with skeletal black armor, crimson cloth and cold portal light.

`pnpm artwork:prepare eredin` creates hashed local WebP cards at 320/640/1280, portraits at 256/512/1024, and transparent 512px figures. Four Riders share one texture and receive stable board-generated numbers and owner-colored pedestals. Rules contain no artwork paths; the bundle compiler separates presentation from engine definitions.
