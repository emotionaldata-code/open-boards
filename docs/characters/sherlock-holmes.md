# Sherlock Holmes

Imported from [UmDb’s Sherlock Holmes deck](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/sherlock-holmes), Cobble & Fog, retrieved 2026-10-25 at 11:55 UTC through its public `/api/db/decks/sherlock-holmes` JSON endpoint. The source supplied no deck or card clarifications. The maintained [inventory](drafts/sherlock-holmes/inventory.json) records all source clauses, quantities, restrictions, values, BOOSTs and image references.

Holmes has 16 health, movement 2 and melee attacks. One Dr. Watson has 8 health and ranged attacks. The deck contains 30 cards across 13 designs. HOLMES maps to MAIN, DR. WATSON to ALLY and ANY to BOTH. Schemes retain their printed BOOST and use engine combat value 0; UmDb’s scheme `value: 1` is a data placeholder, not an attack or defense value.

Deductive Reasoning is represented by the reusable `cardEffectProtection` rule: opposing cancellation cannot suppress MAIN or ALLY card effects, even when Watson plays them. BOTH cards remain cancellable. Protection does not prevent changing values or ignoring an attack. Elementary compares the printed attack value, and its value-ignore operation remains effective against protected effects and surviving BOOSTs. Values are chosen from the engine’s full 0–100 domain, independent of hidden hands or committed cards.

Private inspection and discard choices use the existing authoritative choice protocol. Numeric choices use the same option IDs through a compact value selector. Public face-up defense is projected without exposing the attack. Confirm Suspicion’s required reveal has a public snapshot plus a viewer-safe, acknowledgeable event carrying exactly the revealed cards, so a subsequent command cannot make the reveal unreadable. Continuations remain server-private and survive JSON recovery.

The import preserves all printed effects and quantities. Existing Open Boards base rules remain: five-card initial hand, two actions, hand limit seven, exhaustion, current map, multiplayer elimination and no teams. It is a faithful card import into those base rules, not a replacement implementation of the complete Unmatched rulebook.

## Artwork

Refreshed on 2026-10-26 with original cinematic illustrated artwork generated using Codex’s integrated Imagegen: two portraits, two transparent full-body board figures and one scene for each of the 13 card designs. Holmes retains his deerstalker, charcoal cape, dark teal waistcoat and magnifier; Watson retains his bowler, moustache, brown coat, medical bag and period service revolver. Portraits establish the identities reused by the figures and scenes. Card subjects distinguish investigation, deduction, disguise, pursuit, boxing and medical support.

Selected PNG originals, identity references, exact prompts, source definitions and generation provenance are maintained in `artwork/characters/sherlock-holmes/`. Run `pnpm artwork:prepare sherlock-holmes` to reproduce hashed WebP assets in `apps/client/public/artwork/sherlock-holmes/`, the client manifest and canonical card image paths. Cards ship at 320/640/1280 pixels, portraits at 256/512/1024 and figures on transparent 512-pixel canvases. Figure anchors and scale preserve the existing approximately 128-pixel apparent height. Owner-colored pedestals, ally numbers, selection and animation remain separate from the figure pixels.

The shared artwork and texture loaders render these assets without character-specific branches. Existing local SVG figures and generated card illustrations remain available as load-failure fallbacks. There are no remote runtime artwork dependencies or changes to rules, values, quantities or abilities. Published source images remain reference/provenance URLs in the import inventory and are not redistributed.

Artwork checks cover all 17 assets and 47 derivatives, canonical image references, exact hashes, file sizes, dimensions and real figure alpha. Two repeated preparation runs preserved the manifest entries and derivative filenames. Desktop and landscape-phone browser galleries exercise every card scene and responsive variant. Presentation checks cover four owner colors, numbered Watson figures, selection, movement, removal/restoration and mixed SVG/raster rendering, with only two shared figure texture requests and no browser errors. Restoration is a presentation-harness scenario and does not give Watson a resurrection rule. Selected figure hit targets retain a transparent background so the shared button styling cannot obscure the artwork.
