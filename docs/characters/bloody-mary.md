# Bloody Mary

Imported from [UmDb: Bloody Mary](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/bloody-mary), Battle of Legends, Volume Two, retrieved 2026-10-29. The [unmodified source snapshot](bloody-mary/source.json) retains the API URL and precise retrieval time; the [inventory](bloody-mary/inventory.json) records all printed clauses, values, explicit BOOSTs, restrictions, quantities and source notes. Bloody Mary has 16 HP, movement 3, melee attacks, no allies and 30 cards across 13 designs.

The source's rules are retained without character adaptations. Three Times checks exactly three cards at each owning turn start. Third-action clauses use the actual action ordinal; Closer Than She Appears can grant actions without making the third action ambiguous. Ordinary defense, BOOST, movement and Bloody Reprise do not count as additional actions.

Bloody Reprise captures the original defense before the first combat frame is released. Its second defense is selected afresh and may cancel the copied-value effect with Feint; Feint on Requiem instead prevents the bonus entirely. The original attack is discarded once and its other hooks never repeat. Bonus attacks use the repository's existing continuation convention against the same surviving opposing fighter. The source note for Closer Than She Appears says some printings replace “your fighter” with the fighter's name; this MAIN-only deck yields the same recipient.

The required reusable additions are documented in [the engine guide](../engine.md): action ordinals, all-zone starting-space comparison, retained during-combat bonus values, inspected-card continuations and exact selected-hand discard. Existing generic values, random-discard receipts, BOOSTs, teleports and fighter choices cover the other clauses. No character/card IDs are used in engine branches. Repository-wide conventions such as exhaustion and automatic turn settlement are unchanged.
