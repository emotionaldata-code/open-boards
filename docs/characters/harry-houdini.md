# Harry Houdini import

Source: [UmDb Harry Houdini](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/harry-houdini), retrieved 2026-10-29 through the repository fetch helper. The unmodified payload and provenance are retained in [inventory.json](harry-houdini/inventory.json). Edition: **Houdini vs. The Genie**, design by Sam Crane. The source supplies no edition revision identifier. Stats: 14-HP melee Houdini, movement 2, one 5-HP melee Bess; **30 cards across 13 designs**. Explicit printed BOOSTs, restrictions and every source clause are retained.

The [publisher rulebook](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/UM-HvG_Rules-two-page.pdf) explains the exception for printed BOOST bonuses. The community-maintained, publisher-endorsed Rulings Archive is indexed with its original references by [Gridbeast](https://gridbeast.gg/unmatched-rules-faq/) and [Unstat's Houdini rulings](https://unstat.ru/en/characters/harry-houdini). Those indexed rulings clarify that The Big Reveal requires exactly two simultaneous payments, may recover only one payment made by its own effect, and that Secrets cancels a complete timing step, with later combat timings still resolving. Ordinary effects that merely refer to a discarded card's BOOST do not activate its printed BOOST bonus. Opponent-granted BOOST is a payment rather than an opponent's discard effect for Bess's draw reaction.

Original ruling links (the Discord application itself requires JavaScript/login): [simultaneous selection](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/984104722501886052/1027659212735647845), [own BOOST grant recovery](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/725016882393907241/1064944075196866701), [exactly two payments](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/725016882393907241/1064948695533101126), [whole timing-step cancellation](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/984104722501886052/1019311670499233914), [BOOST versus opponent discard](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/725016882393907241/1069772941207810178), and [restored opposing fighter identity](https://discord.com/channels/722465956265197618/725016882393907241/1100507992232837140). The rendered ruling text was verified through the indexed mirrors; the original Discord message bodies were not accessible in the text browser.

All Part of the Show's restoration ends references to the exact former opposing fighter, as clarified in the archive. Later generic fighter selections can still select that living piece, and references to the opponent's player still draw/discard normally. A deferred zero-health fighter which never restores health is eliminated at combat finish. Subsequent ordinary damage after restoration can defeat it normally. Actual defeats, owner elimination and ally cleanup otherwise retain the shared lifecycle.

Secrets offers its private decision before an inspecting player receives any hidden card options. Revealing it cancels the remaining effects from that source in the current timing step; later timing steps remain. Mandatory discards that do not expose hidden identities, hand-limit discards and BOOST costs bypass inspection protection. Every selection and continuation is serializable through reconnects.

## Conventions and artwork

No character rules were intentionally adapted or omitted. The application retains its shared five-card opening hand, two actions, hand limit seven, exhaustion damage and existing map conventions. Scheme printed values normalize to zero internally. These are shared application conventions, not a claim of complete physical-game equivalence.

All **17 original Imagegen assets** are stored under `artwork/characters/harry-houdini/`: two portraits, two transparent full-body figures and one distinct scene per design. Identity references, exact prompts, source generator paths, hashes and attribution are recorded in `references/`, `prompts.json` and `provenance.json`; `sources.json` records crop/anchor settings. No source game illustrations are redistributed. Browser derivatives were produced with `pnpm artwork:prepare harry-houdini`: 3,477,596 bytes across variants. Both figure masters have real alpha, and the shared renderer retains owner colors and labels.

## Verification

Focused tests cover the complete inventory; restrictions and invalid commands; both combat-role exchanges; simultaneous payments and exact recovery; no-alternative replacement; Bess-only schemes; defense BOOST healing; exact movement recipients; survival for main/ally; hidden inspection cancellation; opponent discard provenance; and JSON choice recovery with hidden projections and forged/wrong-owner rejection. Neutral tests cover payment atomicity, cancellation across timing boundaries, protected inspection, survival fallback, exact former-opponent references versus player ownership, and ability/direct lethal defeat.

The focused run passes **18 fixed-deck tests and 7 neutral engine tests**. Current workspace typecheck, scoped ESLint, scoped Prettier and whitespace checks pass. Italian authored-content coverage was checked; the earlier aggregate localization run reported only the then-in-progress Hamlet entries, with no missing Houdini entries or numeric discrepancies.

**All four focused browser scenarios pass**: the complete 13-design raster gallery/inspectors at desktop and landscape-phone widths, simultaneous BOOST selection and exact recovery with JSON/reload recovery under reduced and full motion, and Houdini/Bess figures across all four owner colors sharing exactly two textures. Keyboard interaction, private opponent projection and original images are covered. The final browser run used `pnpm exec playwright test tests/e2e/harry-houdini.spec.ts tests/e2e/artwork.spec.ts --grep 'Houdini' --output /tmp/houdini-final`; screenshots and transfer receipts are retained under `/tmp/houdini-final`. Desktop and phone owner-color screenshots and all original assets were visually inspected. No page errors or missing Houdini assets were observed.

Full aggregate workspace checks belong to the concurrent import batch and are reported separately. No deployment was performed.
