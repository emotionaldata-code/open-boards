## Reusable engine operations

`CardDefinition.requiresStatus` enforces the current form before attack, defense or scheme commitment through the shared `cardEligible` helper. The same helper controls hand availability and attack replacements. Card fighter labels show the required status's authored name. Form cards remain legal discard and BOOST payments regardless of current form. A legal declaration remains committed when a later effect transforms its actor; suppression cancels pending card transformations normally.

`after-maneuver` fires once after all participating fighters finish ordinary movement and their reactions, before settling the action. `discard.tag` restricts all private/random/payment discard selections to matching printed tags. `draw-and-keep` retains an exact private ordinary-draw receipt across reactions and JSON reconnects, then keeps a chosen subset and bottom-orders the remainder. `take-selected-card` transfers only a bound revealed instance still present in its owning deck; it never substitutes a new top card or invokes draw hooks/exhaustion.

No printed effects are omitted or adapted. Source scheme values of 1 normalize to the engine's action value 0 because schemes have no combat value. Existing project conventions apply: two actions, opening hand five, limit seven, no automatic reshuffle and the shared exhaustion rule. Mr. Hyde's one-per-Maneuver damage follows ordinary prevention/defeat processing. The game's existing solo-hero rendering uses one dual-identity portrait/figure; public form statuses and legal hand controls indicate the active identity.
