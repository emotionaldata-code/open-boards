# Achilles import

Imported from [UmDb: Achilles](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/achilles), retrieved 2026-10-25 through its public JSON API. Edition: **Battle of Legends, Volume Two**. The maintained [source inventory](achilles/inventory.json) records every card, quantity, printed combat/BOOST value, eligible fighter, timed clause, source-art URL and source note. The raw response was audited in temporary storage.

Achilles has 18 HP, movement 2 and melee attacks; his single melee ally Patroclus has 6 HP. The deck preserves **30 copies across 12 designs**. No source card text, quantities or restrictions were adapted. Base Open Boards rules still apply: five-card opening hand, seven-card end-turn limit, shared two-action budget, no automatic reshuffle, two/four-seat free-for-all, and the selected board (not the source set's Hanging Gardens/high ground).

The source's explicit note places Achilles's victory draw after all AFTER COMBAT card effects. The publisher's [Volume Two rules](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/UM-Battle_of_Legends_vol2_Rules-two-page.pdf), bonus-attacks section, supplies the interpretation of Relentless Assault: a separate combat against the same defender even after movement out of range, cancelled when the first card's after-combat effect is suppressed. Achilles draws for a first-combat win before the second attack begins. Both combatants must remain alive.

Defender-first immediate ordering means Under Achilles' Helm can finish its exchange before an attacking Feint resolves; cancellation cannot undo an already resolved exchange. Patroclus's defeat activates grief immediately through the fighter defeat hook; hero defeat and forfeit bypass ally death reactions as in the existing engine. No unsupported printed clauses remain.

## Original artwork

All delivered assets are original code-native SVG illustrations in the repository's cut-paper style. No published source artwork was copied or hotlinked.

- `apps/client/src/characters/art.ts`: Achilles has a high red helmet crest, long spear, round shield and bronze armor; Patroclus has a shorter crest, sword and rectangular shield. Both use a visible seat-colored garment/shield.
- `apps/client/public/cards/achilles/*.svg`: twelve distinct 320×200 compositions, one per source slug. Subjects include paired shields, cracked bronze, a helmet exchange, winged sandals, a vulnerable heel and a flying spear.
- `packages/content/src/characters/achilles.json`: local asset references, bronze/terracotta palette and original flavor lines, separate from executable rules.

The source-art URLs in the inventory are evidence/provenance references only. They are not used by the client.

## Verification

Fixed-deck behavior tests cover all twelve designs, source totals and tags, seeded random discard, defeat and passive timing, bonus-combat cancellation and second defenses, movement out of range, defender exchange and bonus inheritance, private recovery and forged/stale commands, exact recipients, lethal self-payment and forfeit cleanup. Neutral engine fixtures cover the reusable operations independently of Achilles. Existing roster deployment coverage exercises all two/four-seat combinations at full ally counts. Browser checks cover desktop/landscape phone, original card inspectors, private-choice JSON recovery, keyboard input, owner colors and reduced/full motion.

Verification completed with 365 unit/integration tests passing, including 39 Achilles/neutral-operation tests, plus typecheck, lint, formatting and production builds. All 39 browser scenarios passed across the full run and isolated retries; the existing four-player exhaustion test needed a longer timeout, and extra authentication runs required a fresh server with unchanged limits.
