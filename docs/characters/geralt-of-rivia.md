# Geralt of Rivia

Imported from [UmDb’s Geralt of Rivia deck](https://unmatched.cards/umdb/decks/geralt-of-rivia), Steel and Silver, retrieved 2026-10-26. The [maintained inventory](geralt-of-rivia/inventory.json) records all source values, quantities, restrictions, timed clauses, notes and illustration references. UmDb supplies no deck or card clarifications/version number; the raw API snapshot was fetched to temporary working storage.

Geralt has 16 health, movement 2 and melee attacks. Dandelion has 5 health and ranged attacks. All 15 designs and 36 physical copies are included in the authoring pool. Always Prepared chooses one of two swords, one of two armors and one of two potions, retaining both copies of each selected design alongside the 24 base cards. Each of the eight loadouts therefore starts with 30 cards across 12 designs. Unselected gear is removed from the match and cannot be drawn or searched.

The [publisher’s set rules](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/UM-W-SaS-Set-Rules.pdf), pages 3 and 5, supply two details missing from the JSON: Wolf Medallion is the alternate armor; an ongoing scheme activates after its regular effects and replaces the previous ongoing scheme. Gear selection finishes before the opening five-card deal and ally deployment. Each pending selection uses the ordinary private choice/reconnect boundary.

Both potion copies can be played, but only the latest ongoing scheme remains active. An attack by either Geralt or Dandelion satisfies potion upkeep. Defending does not. Ongoing schemes are public in player details and live outside the discard pile; expiration/replacement discards the exact instance with ordinary discard reactions. Maneuver BOOST payments and the seven-card hand limit bypass Tawny Owl because they are not discard effects. Blizzard prevents extra actions rather than the normal two-action turn allotment; in four-seat matches it affects every opposing owner.

Yrden prevents ordinary movement, forced movement, placement and swaps; Stay is legal. Its lock expires at the end of the current turn, including when Geralt played it defensively. Defender-first immediate cancellation can suppress Yrden or Rend before either effect starts. Already resolved effects remain resolved under the shared cancellation rules.

## Shared engine capabilities

`deckSelection` removes unchosen designs from a supplied pool before shuffling/dealing. `boost-combat` supports a card-granted attack or defense BOOST. `lock-movement` lasts through the current turn boundary. `search-cards` filters exact deck/discard instances and can recycle its resolving scheme only on a deck take. `end-turn` finishes queued combat effects, prevents later action refunds from reopening the turn, then runs normal end-step processing. Typed `ongoing` properties implement action-gain prevention, optional discard protection and attack-based expiration. These operations contain no character IDs.

## Differences and artwork

No rules adaptation or omitted source clause is intended. UmDb’s card names/capitalization and all explicit BOOST values are retained. Its Blizzard text says “your fighter”; the card is MAIN-only, and the publisher explicitly names Geralt, so the recipient is identical. The game retains Open Boards’ shared five-card opening hand, seven-card hand limit, exhaustion, two/four-seat map conventions and victory/cancellation timing. This imports a fighter, not the Witcher battlefields.
