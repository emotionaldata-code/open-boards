# Cinematic interface

The access screen, lobby, waiting room and match share a warm charcoal ground, opaque surfaces, ivory text and bronze primary actions. Inter remains the face for rules, controls and numbers; Georgia remains the display serif. No new artwork, dependencies, routes, server APIs, authentication behavior or game rules were introduced by this interface revision. Existing workspace changes were retained.

The access screen answers “How do I join a game?” before showing the fighter gallery on phones. Medusa opens the gallery. Choosing a thumbnail changes the large portrait and statistics; abilities and decks remain available. The gallery stays on the page at every size. Portraits use the registered responsive variants, eager/high-priority loading for the featured image and local SVG fallbacks.

The lobby answers “Which table can I open or join?” Creation and invitation sit alongside the automatically refreshed, paginated room list. The map panorama uses the same artwork registration and vector fallback as the arena. Room rows keep host, mode, occupancy and admission separate, including on phones. Loading, empty and failed requests keep the creation/invitation controls available.

The waiting room answers “Who is ready, and what remains before starting?” Seats show portraits and readiness. Character selection, invitation, readiness and host start controls remain visible through ordinary page scrolling. Clipboard success and failure are announced. A narrow character picker puts its action on a separate row.

The match answers “Whose turn is it, and what should I do next?” Players and turn sit above the arena; contextual instructions and cards sit below it, or alongside it on landscape phones. Active players have a bronze frame and an accessible current-step marker. Compact discard controls leave more space for player names and health on phones. The match keeps a fixed viewport height; outside-match pages can scroll vertically. Board geometry, zones, portals, targets and direct interactions are preserved.

## Design review

The `design-taste-frontend` app direction was **True-black flat**, seed **637032500**, chosen for its opaque surfaces and quiet chrome. The requested charcoal/ivory/bronze palette, existing two typefaces and 44px touch targets take precedence over the sheet's white 36px primary button. No direction redraw was needed. The product's own fighter portraits and King Solomon's Mine supply its identity; the live element is the manually selected fighter gallery. There is no invented production data or external stock imagery.

Browser inspection of the access screen at 1440, 768 and 390px finishes with **0 failures and 0 warnings**, allowing `palette-cream` for the explicitly requested ivory text and `app-title-size` for the access screen's display title. Source design lint has no failures; its one shadow warning is the existing floating dialog, where a shadow is appropriate.

The screenshot review corrected three issues: incomplete thumbnail labels at the phone edge, a squeezed tablet character picker, and card text clipped by extra landscape padding. Final review checks the main action first, aligned content edges, consistent functional colors, content-derived character statistics, honest empty/loading/error states, unchanged map markings, and phone access to every control. Desktop, tablet, portrait phone and landscape phone captures are generated by `tests/e2e/cinematic.spec.ts`; broader match geometry checks also cover 320×568 through 3840×2160.

Measured contrast ratios: body text on panels **14.63:1**, muted text on raised surfaces **7.16:1**, primary button text **8.07:1**, focus ring against controls **7.27:1**, and errors on panels **9.94:1**. Reduced motion disables CSS transitions and animations. Existing native dialogs retain Escape handling and return keyboard focus to their trigger.
