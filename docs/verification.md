## Visual direction

The current fantasy UI and its component boundaries are described in [ui.md](ui.md). Browser checks cover viewport fit on sign-in, lobby, waiting rooms and active matches, compact player/discard dialogs and transient shared action effects, keyboard focus restoration, login-only rules, fullscreen entry/exit, desktop matches and landscape phone matches. Card hands may scroll horizontally; long dialog contents scroll within their dialog.

King Solomon’s Mine has 29 spaces, seven zones and six movement-only portals with a fixed full-board view and no legend; the wider layout keeps movement visible on player badges and in their details. Presentation tests cover public draw notifications without private card data, hidden attack commitment, revealed melee/ranged combat, duplicate suppression, ordering, and cancellation. Browser checks verify shared movement/attack effects across independent clients, plus full-motion ranged combat and reduced-motion matches.

This is a local repository deliverable; deployment was not requested. Matches intentionally do not survive a server restart. No recovery, email verification, refresh tokens, teams or a character editor is implemented.
