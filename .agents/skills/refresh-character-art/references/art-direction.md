# Shared art direction

Use cinematic illustrated realism: credible anatomy and materials, depth, dramatic motivated light and readable silhouettes. Retain existing UI layouts, parchment and restrained palette. Choose narrative subjects specific to each card, with varied camera distance and staging.

Mythological subjects favor weathered limestone, bronze patina, individually resolved scales/feathers, atmospheric depth and a solemn threatening mood. Medusa's pilot identity is olive skin, angular adult face, amber eyes, living dark green snake crown, antique bronze armor, dark teal cloth and recurved bronze bow. Harpies have fierce faces, black feather crests, charcoal wings and avian talons. Other characters retain their own eras, equipment and identity.

Portraits show clear faces and upper silhouettes; transparent full-body figures retain complete extremities, with no baked pedestal or owner color. Card scenes use 8:5 framing with crucial action inside the central 80%. Reuse identity references. No embedded text, numbers, frames, icons, logos or watermarks: UI owns them. Aim for realistic painted illustration rather than cut-paper vectors, plastic miniatures or photographic costumes.
