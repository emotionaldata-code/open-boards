# Open Boards sound library

Recorded sound effects by Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl), licensed CC0 1.0.
Original license files are included alongside the recordings.

- Casino Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio — paper cards, chips and stacks.
- RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio — leather, cloth, footsteps, books and weapons.
- Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds — wood, metal, glass, bell and body impacts.
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Downloaded 2026-10-29. Original Ogg recordings are bundled locally, without runtime
third-party requests. Playback recipes in `src/audio/cues.ts` layer recordings with
small pitch and stereo variations. A short room response and compressor in
`src/audio/mix.ts` control depth and overlapping transients. No oscillator UI beeps.

`fire-crackle.ogg` is a 1.1-second excerpt with edge fades from **Fire Crackling**
by AntumDeluge, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling/
Original: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/fire-1_0.ogg
